# NMAM  Institute of Technology - Examination Guidelines (End-Semester Examination, SEE)

## 1. Hall Ticket
- Hall tickets are released on the ERP portal 10 days before the first exam.
- Only students with 75% attendance (or approved condonation) and cleared fee dues receive a hall ticket.
- Students must carry a printed hall ticket and the college ID card to every exam. Without these, entry is not permitted; a duplicate hall ticket can be obtained from the Examination Cell for Rs. 200.

## 2. Reporting and Timing
- Report to the exam hall 30 minutes before the start time. Seats are displayed on the notice board 45 minutes before the exam.
- Late entry is allowed up to 30 minutes after the exam begins. After that, the student cannot write the exam.
- A student cannot leave the hall during the first 60 minutes and must not leave without handing over the answer booklet.
- Theory exams are 3 hours long and carry 100 marks. Lab practical exams are 2 hours.

## 3. Allowed and Prohibited Items
- Allowed: hall ticket, ID card, blue or black pens, pencils, geometry box, and a non-programmable scientific calculator (Casio fx-991 series) in courses where the question paper permits it.
- Prohibited: mobile phones, smartwatches, earphones, programmable calculators, notes, books and any written material. Phones must be switched off and left with bags outside the hall.
- Water bottles must be transparent with labels removed.

## 4. Answer Booklet Instructions
- Write the register number only in the space provided. Any identifying mark or name results in a malpractice case.
- Begin each answer on a fresh page and strike out unused pages. Rough work must be done on the last page and crossed out.
- Additional sheets must be tied to the main booklet with the supplied thread.

## 5. Malpractice Rules
- Possession of prohibited material: the answer script for that paper is cancelled and a written warning is issued.
- Copying or exchanging material: the student receives zero in that paper and is barred from the next paper in the same session.
- Impersonation or repeated offence: all papers of the semester are cancelled and the case is referred to the Disciplinary Committee, which may suspend or rusticate the student.
- Reports are filed by the invigilator and reviewed by the Controller of Examinations within 7 days.

## 6. Special Provisions
- Students with benchmark disabilities receive 20 extra minutes per hour of exam time and may use a scribe with prior approval from the Examination Cell, requested at least 7 days before the exam.
- If a student falls ill during the exam period, a medical certificate must be submitted within 48 hours. Missed papers may be written in the supplementary examination without a late fee.
- If a student is absent due to a clash of two exams, the Examination Cell will schedule a special slot within the same week.

## 7. Supplementary Examination
- Supplementary exams for odd-semester courses are held in February.
- Registration opens within 7 days of the result declaration and requires the fee of Rs. 1,000 per theory paper.
- Students with grade F in a lab course must reappear for the lab practical in the supplementary session.
