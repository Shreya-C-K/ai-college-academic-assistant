# NMAM  Institute of Technology - Student FAQs

## Campus Services
Q: How do I get a bonafide certificate?
A: Apply on the ERP portal under Student Services. The certificate is issued within 2 working days from the Admin Office. The fee is Rs. 50.

Q: I lost my ID card. What should I do?
A: Report the loss to the Admin Office and pay Rs. 300 for a duplicate ID card. It is issued in 3 working days. Carry the receipt until the new card arrives.

Q: What are the library timings and borrowing rules?
A: The central library is open 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. The late fine is Rs. 2 per day per book. Books can be renewed once if no one has reserved them.

Q: How do I get my transcript?
A: Apply on the ERP portal. An official transcript costs Rs. 500 per copy and is delivered in 5 working days.

Q: What is the Wi-Fi policy?
A: Each student gets Wi-Fi access with their ERP login on up to 2 devices. Streaming sites are throttled during class hours. For issues contact helpdesk@NMAM -tech.example.

## Fees and Scholarships
Q: What is the last date to pay semester fees and what is the late fee?
A: Fees must be paid within the first 3 weeks of the semester. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after that, up to a maximum of Rs. 3,000.

Q: Are scholarships available?
A: Yes. The Merit Scholarship gives a 25% tuition waiver to students with SGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous semester. Government scholarships can be applied for through the Scholarship Cell, Admin Block, Room 108.

## Academics
Q: Can I change my elective after registration?
A: Only within the first 2 weeks of the semester through the ERP portal, subject to seat availability and the Head of Department's approval.

Q: What if I miss an Internal Assessment?
A: If the reason is genuine (medical or official duty), apply to the Controller of Examinations within 7 days with documents for a re-test. Only one IA re-test per semester is allowed.

Q: What is the minimum number of credits I should register for?
A: A minimum of 20 credits and a maximum of 28 credits per semester.

Q: What happens if my attendance is less than 75%?
A: You may apply for condonation if attendance is between 65% and 74.9%, with a valid reason and a fee of Rs. 500 per course. Below 65% you are detained in that course.

Q: Can I get my answer script re-checked?
A: Yes. Apply for revaluation within 5 working days of the result (Rs. 800 per paper). Photocopies cost Rs. 400.

Q: Is there a grievance redressal mechanism?
A: Yes. Submit a complaint on the ERP grievance portal or email grievance@NMAM -tech.example. The committee responds within 7 working days.

## Student Life
Q: Which student clubs are available?
A: NMAM  Coding Club, AI and Robotics Society, Cultural Club (Nrityam), Photography Club, Entrepreneurship Cell and the NSS unit. Registration happens in the second week of every semester.

Q: Is counselling available?
A: Yes. The Student Wellness Centre (Block C, Room 12) offers free confidential counselling Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 5 PM. Book through the ERP portal or walk in.

Q: What are the hostel in-time rules?
A: Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM for first and second years and 10:30 PM for third and fourth years. Late entry requires warden approval.

Q: How can I contact the Academic Office?
A: Email academics@NMAM -tech.example or visit Admin Block, Room 101, between 9:30 AM and 4:30 PM on working days.
