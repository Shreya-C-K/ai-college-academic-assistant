# NMAM  Institute of Technology - Internship and Placement Guidelines

## 1. Internship Requirement
- Every B.Tech student must complete a mandatory internship of at least 8 weeks (minimum 320 working hours) during the summer break between Semester 6 and Semester 7.
- The internship carries 4 credits under course code INT701 and is graded on a Pass/Fail basis with a score of at least 50 out of 100.
- The internship must be in a field related to the student's branch (software, data, AI/ML, networking, cybersecurity, embedded systems and similar).

## 2. Eligibility and Approval
- Students become eligible after completing Semester 5 with no more than 2 active backlogs.
- The offer letter must be submitted to the Training and Placement Cell (TPC) and approved by the assigned faculty mentor before the internship starts. Internships started without approval will not be counted.
- The offer letter must be on company letterhead and include the role, start and end dates, and supervisor contact details.
- Offers from companies, startups, research labs (IITs, IISc, NITs) and government organizations are accepted. Remote internships are allowed with prior approval, up to a maximum of 4 weeks of the 8-week total being remote.
- The deadline to submit the internship offer letter is 28 February 2027.

## 3. Stipend and Duration
- A stipend is not mandatory. Students may do paid or unpaid internships.
- Internships longer than 8 weeks are allowed and encouraged, but only 8 weeks count towards the credit.

## 4. Documentation
- Weekly logbook: a short report signed by the industry supervisor every week.
- Final report: 30 to 50 pages including problem statement, tools used, work done, results and learning outcomes, with a plagiarism score below 20%.
- Completion certificate from the organization, with dates matching the offer letter.
- The report and certificate must be submitted within 10 days of Semester 7 starting.

## 5. Evaluation (100 Marks)
- Industry supervisor's evaluation: 40 marks
- Faculty mentor's evaluation: 20 marks
- Final report: 20 marks
- Presentation and viva (15 minutes): 20 marks
A student who fails the internship must repeat it in the next summer break.

## 6. Placement Eligibility
- Campus placements are open to students with a minimum CGPA of 6.5 and no active backlogs.
- A student who accepts a Dream offer (CTC above Rs. 12 lakh) becomes ineligible for further companies, except for super-dream offers.
- Placement training (aptitude, coding and soft skills) is conducted in Semesters 5 and 6, and attendance of 80% in these sessions is mandatory for registering with the TPC.
