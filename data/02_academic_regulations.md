# NMAM  Institute of Technology - Academic Regulations (B.Tech, Regulation 2024)

## 1. Attendance
- A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course (theory and lab, counted separately) to be eligible to write the End-Semester Examination (SEE).
- Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% in a course may apply for condonation. Condonation requires a valid reason (medical or family emergency), supporting documents, approval from the Head of the Department, and a condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course.
- Students with attendance below 65% are detained in that course. They receive an "NE" (Not Eligible) status and must repeat the course in the next offering.
- Medical certificates must be submitted to the class advisor within 7 days of resuming classes.
- Participation in official activities (sports, NSS, hackathons, technical fests) with prior written approval is counted as attendance, up to a maximum of 10% of classes.

## 2. Evaluation Scheme
- Theory courses: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) carries 50 marks and the SEE carries 50 marks (the 3-hour paper of 100 marks is scaled down to 50).
- CIE split for theory: Internal Assessment 1 (15 marks), Internal Assessment 2 (15 marks), assignments (10 marks), quizzes (10 marks).
- Lab courses: CIE of 50 marks (record, weekly performance, viva) and an SEE practical exam of 50 marks.
- To pass a course a student must score at least 20 out of 50 in the SEE and at least 50 out of 100 overall.
- A student who misses one Internal Assessment for a genuine reason (medical or official duty) may apply to the Controller of Examinations within 7 days for a re-test, with documents.

## 3. Grading System
| Marks (out of 100) | Grade | Grade Points |
|---|---|---|
| 90 - 100 | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80 - 89 | A+ | 9 |
| 70 - 79 | A | 8 |
| 60 - 69 | B+ | 7 |
| 55 - 59 | B | 6 |
| 50 - 54 | C | 5 |
| Below 50 | F (Fail) | 0 |
| Absent / detained | AB / NE | 0 |

## 4. SGPA and CGPA
- SGPA = sum of (credits x grade points) for all courses in the semester divided by the sum of credits of those courses.
- CGPA = the same calculation across all completed semesters.
- A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required for the award of the B.Tech degree.
- Courses with grade F are excluded from credits earned but their credits count in SGPA calculation until cleared.

## 5. Backlogs and Supplementary Exams
- A student who gets grade F (backlog) must clear it through the supplementary examination held after the next semester's results, or by re-registering for the course.
- A student can carry a maximum of 4 active backlogs into Semester 7.
- The supplementary exam fee is Rs. 1,000 per theory paper and Rs. 600 per lab.
- The maximum duration to complete the B.Tech programme is 6 years from the date of admission.

## 6. Revaluation and Photocopy of Answer Scripts
- A student can apply for a photocopy of an answer script within 5 working days of result declaration. Fee: Rs. 400 per paper.
- Revaluation can be requested within the same 5 working days. Fee: Rs. 800 per paper.
- Revaluation results are announced within 15 working days. If the marks change, the higher score is awarded.

## 7. Course Registration and Electives
- Every student must register for a minimum of 20 and a maximum of 28 credits in a semester through the ERP portal.
- Elective changes are permitted only during the first 2 weeks of the semester, subject to seat availability.
- Course withdrawal is allowed until the end of Week 6 with the Head of Department's approval; a withdrawn course appears as "W" on the transcript.

## 8. Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism in assignments, reports or projects results in zero marks for that component. A repeat offence leads to a course fail and a disciplinary hearing.
- Use of AI tools is allowed for learning but any submitted work must be the student's own and AI usage must be declared where the course instructor requires it.
